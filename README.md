### CHEM-000231: Sharp three-dimensional Lieb–Thirring kinetic constant
- **Subfield:** Round 2
- **Open:** Determine the optimal three-dimensional fermionic kinetic-energy constant, equivalently the sharp Lieb–Thirring spectral constant at \(\gamma=1\). Resolve whether the semiclassical value is optimal.
- **Status:** Open since the 1970s. The semiclassical constant was proved for shifted Coulomb Hamiltonians in 2025; arbitrary admissible potentials remain unresolved.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Sharpens electronic kinetic-energy lower bounds and quantitative stability estimates for matter.
- **Key works:** T. C. Corso and T. Ried (2025), *On a Variational Problem Related to the Cwikel–Lieb–Rozenblum and Lieb–Thirring Inequalities*, [DOI:10.1007/s00220-024-05216-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-024-05216-y); Corso, T. Weidl and Z. Zeng (2025), *Lieb–Thirring inequalities for the shifted Coulomb Hamiltonian*, [DOI:10.4171/JST/574](https://doi.org/10.4171/JST/574).
- **Related:** Analysis: sharp functional inequalities; mathematical physics: stability of matter.
